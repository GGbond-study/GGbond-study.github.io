I am currently a Master's student at Beihang University (Sept. 2024 – present), fortunate to be advised by Prof. Lijiang Chen[https://shi.buaa.edu.cn/buaaclj/zh_CN/index/28500], Prof. Qi Zhao[https://shi.buaa.edu.cn/07297/zh_CN/index/48838], and Dr. Shuchang Lv[https://cv-shuchanglyu.github.io]. I received my Bachelor's degree from South China Agricultural University in 2024. My current research centers on enhancing reasoning capabilities in Large Language Models (LLMs) and Vision-Language Models (VLMs), with a growing interest in reinforcement learning. Previously, I explored federated learning, multi-modal medical image analysis and applications of vision foundation models.
