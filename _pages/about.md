I am currently a Master's student at Beihang University (Sept. 2024 – present), fortunate to be advised by Prof. Lijiang Chen, Prof. Qi Zhao, and [Dr. Shuchang Lyu](https://cv-shuchanglyu.github.io). I received my Bachelor's degree from South China Agricultural University in 2024. My current research centers on enhancing reasoning capabilities in Large Language Models (LLMs) and Vision-Language Models (VLMs), with a growing interest in reinforcement learning. Previously, I explored federated learning, multi-modal medical image analysis and applications of vision foundation models.
